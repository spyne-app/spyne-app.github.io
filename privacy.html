About Privacy

Privacy

Spyne is a personal project. It does not run advertising or analytics and does not track people.

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

What the app stores

Spyne stores a catalogue of items the user owns: titles, barcodes, editions, ratings, viewing status, cover images and, optionally, the user's own photos of covers. This data is kept in a database hosted by Supabase (in Singapore) and in a local copy on the user's phone. The app currently has no public sign-up and is used by its maintainer.

What is sent to other services

To fill in details about an item, the app sends lookup requests to third-party services. These contain the item's barcode, ISBN, title, year or director, not personal information about the user.

  • TMDB, Google Books, Open Library and UPCitemdb: barcodes, ISBNs and titles, to retrieve metadata and covers.
  • Wikidata and Wikipedia: titles and identifiers, to retrieve public information such as credits.
  • Google Gemini: when a barcode scan fails, a photo of the cover can be sent to read its details, and a film's title, year and director can be sent to produce a short synopsis.

Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy.

This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. As with any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when pages are requested; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Requests and contact

To ask what data is held, or to have it deleted, write to spyne.app.contact@gmail.com.

If the app is ever released to other users, this policy will be updated before that happens.
