About Privacy

A catalogue for a shelf full of physical media

Spyne is a personal, non-commercial Android app for cataloguing a collection of Blu-rays, DVDs, books and CDs by scanning their barcodes.

What it is

Spyne keeps a library of owned items with edition-accurate covers, ratings and viewing status, synced to a small online database so the collection can be browsed on the phone. It is built as a personal tool. It is not available on any app store, and there is no public sign-up.

Data sources and attribution

Spyne looks up public information about the items it catalogues. The sources are credited here.

  • Wikidata: structured data, released under CC0.
  • Wikipedia: where content derived from Wikipedia is shown, it is credited to Wikipedia and its contributors and available under CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB): film and series metadata and images. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Google Books and Open Library: book metadata and covers.
  • UPCitemdb: barcode lookups.

How Spyne uses Wikimedia services

Spyne makes low-volume requests to the Wikidata and Wikipedia APIs, caches what it retrieves, and is designed to respect rate limits. Its requests identify the project with this User-Agent:

Spyne/0.1 (https://spyne-app.github.io; spyne.app.contact@gmail.com)
